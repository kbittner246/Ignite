Privacy Policy

Recipe Sharing & Lists

Last updated: May 29, 2026

Overview

Recipe Sharing & Lists does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All recipes, grocery lists, photos, and settings are stored locally on your device.

Photos

The app accesses your photo library and camera solely to attach images to your recipes. Photos are saved locally on your device and are never uploaded or shared with third parties.

Recipe Sharing

When you share a recipe, the .recipe file is shared through your chosen method (Messages, Mail, AirDrop, etc.). We do not have access to shared content.

Website Parsing

When you provide a recipe URL, the app fetches the webpage directly from your device to extract ingredients and directions. No data is sent to our servers or any third party.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at support@igniteapplications.com
