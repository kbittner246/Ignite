Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 3, 2026

Recipe Sharing & Lists ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy.

Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All recipe data, grocery lists, and photos are stored locally on your device.

Camera Usage

The App requests camera access solely to allow you to take photos of recipes. These photos are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded or shared without your explicit action.

Recipe Sharing

When you choose to share a recipe, the recipe data is encoded directly into a URL link. No data is stored on any server. The recipient decodes the recipe data directly from the link.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please visit igniteapplications.com.
